    Hi, I'm Vibhat Srivastava 👋

    An Infrastructure Automation Architect, Agentic AI Developer, and Senior DevOps Engineer with 14+ years of hands-on experience architecting, developing, and operating mission-critical enterprise systems and intelligent automation platforms.

    Over the last decade and a half, I have spearheaded infrastructure transformations from manual, ticket-driven operations to modern, highly reliable, and self-healing cloud ecosystems. As the architect and founder behind Server Automation Tech, I design and develop custom tools, developer platforms, and Agentic AI workflows. Most recently, I released Network Control Plane (v1.0.0-GA), an open-source, provider-neutral control plane for container and multi-cloud workload networking with declarative GitOps drift reconciliation and governed AI diagnostics.
